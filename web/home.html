Fingertips

Your hands. Your voice. One place for Deaf, hard-of-hearing and nonspeaking people to call, meet, learn and play. Built at HackUMass XIV.

Call

Phone calls

Call any phone number from the browser. Their words appear as live captions. You answer in ASL, by tapping or by typing, and Fingertips speaks your reply. Phone number: +1 (878) 877-6285.

Meet

Video calls and Google Meet

Start a video call, send the link to anyone, and sign. Your sentence is spoken into the call with a Gemini voice and shown as a caption. The Chrome extension does the same inside Google Meet.

Start a video call Meet extension

Extension: unzip, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked.

Learn

Learn any hands-on task

Gemini watches a phone video of a task and turns it into a step-by-step lesson: every step, which hand does what, and where it happens.

Play

Sign2Speak JAM

Make music with your hands. Fingers pick the chord, movement plays it, and every note is shown as light and felt as a vibration. Invite friends into the same room.
