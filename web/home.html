Four ways to be heard.

01 · CALL

Phone calls

Call any number from your browser. Their words become live captions. You sign, tap or type, and they hear your voice. Or call us: +1 (878) 877-6285.

Open phone calls→
02 · MEET

Video calls & Google Meet

Start a video call and share the link. Your signed sentence is spoken into the call with a Gemini voice and captioned on your camera. The Chrome extension does it inside Google Meet.

Start a video call→ Meet extension
Unzip → chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked
03 · LEARN

Learn any task

Gemini watches a phone video of a hands-on task and turns it into a lesson: every step, which hand does what, and where it happens.

See it in action→ Code
04 · PLAY

Sign2Speak JAM

Your hands are the instrument. Fingers pick the chord, movement plays it, and every note glows on screen and buzzes in your hand. Invite friends to the same room.
